1. Project Purpose

The project is the official digital business platform for A TO Z and SIGNAL mosquito coil brands. It combines a public business website, product/content CMS, media library, mini lead CRM, SEO controls and a maintenance center.

  • Present brands, products, specifications, safety instructions and catalogues.
  • Collect distributor and product enquiries throughout Bangladesh.
  • Manage leads, notes, follow-ups, duplicates and conversions.
  • Publish company news, sales meets, launches and archives.
  • Run on conventional PHP/MySQL shared hosting without Node.js.

10. Backlink Strategy

  • Complete consistent business profiles with the same name, phone, address and canonical homepage.
  • Request relevant links from manufacturers, brand principals, distributors and retail partners.
  • Publish useful mosquito-awareness, safe-use, product and distribution articles.
  • Seek relevant Bangladesh trade-directory, local-news and event mentions.
  • Link to the most relevant product/article page, not always the homepage.
  • Avoid paid link farms, automated comments, irrelevant directories and anchor spam.

Backlinks are ongoing off-site work and cannot be marked Done from source code alone.

The genuine remaining work is deployment configuration, live verification, README/catalogue/OG/Search Console completion, POST-only admin mutations, safer media deletion and measured performance/mobile/database testing.
